# SS TOKYO DRESSES — Project Brief

## Brand and experience

- **Store name:** SS TOKYO DRESSES
- **Website:** sstokyodresses.store
- **Look and feel:** Modern, trendy, clean fashion shopping experience inspired by Myntra.
- **Colour direction:** Baby pink and maroon, with warm neutrals and black for contrast.
- **Typography:** Modern and clean.
- **Logo:** Existing SS TOKYO DRESSES logo supplied. Its colour may be adapted to harmonise with the website palette.

## Audience and catalogue

- **Customer:** Working women aged 25–40.
- **Category:** Women’s ethnic blouses.
- **Material and origin:** Vichitra silk, Made in India.
- **Product presentation:** Each design uses a selected cover photograph and four matching gallery photographs. Use the supplied sizing guide.

### Masoba Blouse

## Pages and features

- Home, Shop, category/product pages, search, Cart/Checkout, About, Contact.
- Size guide, offers banner, customer reviews, and customer-support section.
- Opening promotion: **30% off the first order**.

## Order fulfilment and support

- **Payments:** UPI and Cash on Delivery. Orders are submitted by email to customer support.
- **Delivery area:** Anywhere in India.
- **Shipping:** ₹60 below ₹1,799; free above ₹1,799.
- **Returns/exchanges:** Not offered.
- **Support email:** sstokyodresses.customersupport@gmail.com
